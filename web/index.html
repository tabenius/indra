More than a transport

Start with people.
Build the means to connect.

For communities facing censorship, exclusion, displacement, or repeated loss of resources, communication is one part of a larger need: to find peers, preserve knowledge, and shape a life together.

01

Nearby, not dependent.

Explore device-to-device communication when ordinary infrastructure is unavailable. Design from real constraints: power, range, cost, accessibility, and time.

02

Continuity, not just delivery.

Ask what happens after a lost phone, a broken link, or a long absence. Identity recovery and belonging deserve as much attention as a successful packet.

03

Evidence, not blind trust.

Keep intent, source, uncertainty, and review visible. Carry the human–AI trust principles explored by Nostoi and WeftMark into the way we work.

The development commitment

A second net.
A shared promise.

Indra begins with OSHI's published core. The goal is to preserve its ordinary network compatibility while adding a separate place to experiment.

Two profiles are planned. Isolation and interoperability still need implementation and real-device verification.
