# Jewel Commons

A dome holds space without closing it. Its low, generous silhouette suggests a
shelter made from relationships rather than walls. The form is meticulously
constructed around a single vertical axis, with enough negative space that each
connection can be read independently.

The net has a restrained rhythm: a few large diamonds, not a fine decorative
lattice. Precision comes from consistent junctions and balanced proportions.
Painstaking attention to those junctions lets a small mark carry the same idea
as a large one; detail is removed before recognition is sacrificed.

Deep teal is the structural ink; warm gold distinguishes the jewels. Paper is
a supporting ground, never another competing ornament. Flat fields avoid the
fragility of gradients, transparency stacks, or faux-metallic highlights. Facets
are suggested with one cut, allowing the eye to complete the polyhedron.

The visual hierarchy begins with the whole shelter, then the crossing threads,
then the individual jewels. This master-level restraint means the mark needs
no caption to feel connected. Words, when present, occupy their own space and
never interfere with the geometry.

Craft is demonstrated through reduction and repeatability. A mono version,
a compact favicon, and a full launcher tile share the same silhouette, while
respecting the different distances from which they are seen. The finished system
should feel deliberate, quiet, and available to everyone.
